## External Signals (Researcher)

- Ultralytics exposes the requested training controls as direct train settings, including `mosaic`, `scale`, `translate`, HSV augmentation controls, flips, `freeze`, `weight_decay`, and `warmup_epochs`.
- For small UAV objects, lower geometry augmentation is a good first controlled experiment: reduce mosaic, scale, and translation before adding epochs.
- For class-confusion reduction, reduce appearance-changing transforms first: lower HSV jitter, keep `degrees` at `0.0`, and use `flipud 0.0`.
- Use validation artifacts to decide whether augmentation helped: confusion matrix, PR curves, per-class precision/recall, and small-object false negatives.
- Treat `freeze=10` as a transfer-learning control for small/noisy datasets, not as the default final recipe.
